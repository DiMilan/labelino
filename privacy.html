Privacy Policy

Labelino for Mac · Last updated: 1 November 2026

In short: Labelino does not collect, store or share any personal data on our side. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no servers of our own. Everything the app keeps stays on your Mac.

Who is responsible

Labelino is developed by Milan Dima, Belgium (the “developer”, “we”). For any privacy question or request, contact 2ndchatapp@gmail.com.

Bluetooth

Labelino uses Bluetooth only to find and talk to your label printer. The label you design is sent directly from your Mac to the printer. Nothing is sent over the internet. You can withdraw Bluetooth access at any time in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Bluetooth.

Data stored on your Mac

To pick up where you left off, the app stores on your Mac only:

  • your last label design (text, font, size, QR code content and a reduced copy of an image you added);
  • print settings such as darkness and number of copies;
  • an identifier of your printer, so it can reconnect automatically.

These never leave your Mac and are removed when you delete the app.

Files and images

When you choose or drop an image, Labelino reads only that file, with your permission through macOS. It does not access any other files.

Tracking

The app does not track you across apps or websites, contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, and makes no network connections.

Your rights (GDPR)

Because we do not process personal data on our side, there is normally nothing for us to access, correct or delete; all app data is on your Mac and is deleted with the app. You can still contact us with any question at the address above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority (Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit / Autorité de protection des données).

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
